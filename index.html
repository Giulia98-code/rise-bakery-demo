About Rise Bakery

We are a dedicated artisan micro-bakery rooted in the vibrant heart of Amsterdam's De Pijp neighborhood. Our philosophy is simple: time, patience, and uncompromising ingredients.

Every loaf of sourdough undergoes an extended slow fermentation process to unlock deep, complex flavors and ensure maximum digestibility. We source organic stone-milled flours and authentic European butter to craft pastries that honor traditional French techniques with a modern Amsterdam soul.

The Hidden Gem!!

We are located just behind the market stalls and therefore we refer to ourselves as the hidden gem as you need to know us to find us, and when you do, you will be pleasantly surprised :)

One of the most popular markets of Amsterdam, Albert Cuypstraat Market. I am sure you have already heard of it!

The Albert Cuyp is in the heart of De Pijp, a vibrant 19th-century neighborhood and has evolved into one of the most lively areas of Amsterdam.

The market is the biggest in Europe, and it has such a great atmosphere.

Shopping on an empty stomach is never fun so don't forget to stop by us for a quick bite before continuing down the street.
